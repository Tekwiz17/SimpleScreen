# SimpleScreen 1.1.0

## A much bigger desktop collection

27 widget types, up from 8: Device Batteries, World Clock, Analog Clock, Countdown, Stopwatch, Checklist, Habit, Water, Breathe, Daily Thought, Day Progress, Storage, Memory, Uptime, Quick Links, Calculator, Dice, Color Studio, and Weather Details join the originals. Search the catalog and customize each widget in place.

## Wallpaper motion fixed

- Native 30 fps redraw timer replaces reliance on SwiftUI's background animation schedule.
- Gradient now actually animates. Existing designs have more visible motion.
- New Ripple, Starlight, and Ribbons designs bring the procedural collection to eight.
- Animate on battery, Low Power Mode, and Reduce Motion are separate controls. The studio explains exactly why motion is paused.
- Pausing retains the current phase; resuming continues it.

## Connected-device batteries

Device Batteries shows this Mac and connected Bluetooth accessories reported by macOS. AirPods left/right/case appear separately when available; keyboards, mice, trackpads, and other accessories use their exposed readings. Unknown levels show a dash, never an invented percentage. Updates occur every two minutes or on demand.

This does not promise universal iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, or unsupported-accessory battery access. No private Apple account APIs or credentials are used.
